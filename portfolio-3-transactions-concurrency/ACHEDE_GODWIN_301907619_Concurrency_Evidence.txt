=== DEMO 2: LOST UPDATE (READ COMMITTED, read-then-write, no lock) ===
[A] read stock = 10
[B] read stock = 10
[B] wrote stock = read value - 4
[A] wrote stock = read value - 3
FINAL stock of product 1 = 7   <-- WRONG: correct answer is 10-3-4 = 3 (A overwrote B)

=== DEMO 3: FIX WITH SELECT ... FOR UPDATE (row lock) ===
[A] locked row, read stock = 10
[A] wrote stock = read value - 3
[B] locked row, read stock = 7
[B] wrote stock = read value - 4
FINAL stock of product 1 = 3   <-- CORRECT: 10-3-4 = 3 (B waited for A)

=== DEMO 4: REPEATABLE READ detects the conflict ===
[A] read stock = 10
[B] read stock = 10
psql:04_repeatable_read_A.sql:7: ERROR:  could not serialize access due to concurrent update
FINAL stock of product 1 = 6   <-- only B committed (10-4); A was rejected and must retry
